## 24K vs 22K — Which Should You Buy?

| Factor              | 24K (999/999.9)                  | 22K (916)                        |
|---------------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Purity**          | 99.9% pure gold                  | 91.6% gold + 8.4% alloy         |
| **Price per gram**  | ₹15,017                         | ₹13,765                         |
| **Best for**        | Investment, resale, bars         | Jewellery, daily-wear coins      |
| **Resale value**    | Higher (universal benchmark)     | Lower (alloy reduces value)      |
| **Durability**      | Softer, scratches easily         | Harder, more durable             |
| **Making charges**  | 0% on bars                       | 3-25% on jewellery/coins         |
| **Liquidity**       | Easy (buyback guaranteed)        | Depends on jeweller              |
| **GST**             | 3%                               | 3%                               |

### Verdict: Buy 24K bars for investment. Buy 22K only for wearable jewellery.

For pure investment, 24K bars give you the highest gold content per rupee spent and the best resale value. 22K coins/bars contain 8.4% non-gold alloy, so you're paying for metal that isn't gold.

## Key Tips for Buying Gold Bars Cheaply

1. **Buy bars, not jewellery** — Zero making charges vs 8-25% on jewellery
2. **Compare across platforms** — Same MMTC-PAMP bar can be ₹2,000+ cheaper on Myntra vs Amazon
3. **Buy on dips** — Gold dropped ₹251/g today; watch for corrections
4. **Check BIS hallmark** — Ensures purity; essential for resale value
5. **Buy online** — Often cheaper than physical stores (lower overhead)
6. **Avoid small denominations** — 1g coins carry 5-10% premium; 10g+ bars are best value
7. **Buy in bulk** — Per-gram premiums drop on larger bars (50g, 100g)
8. **Avoid Delhi** — Marginally higher rates vs southern/western cities
